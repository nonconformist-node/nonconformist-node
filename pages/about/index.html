About

Table of Contents

Ideas in my mind are amorphous and non-linear, non-sequential, disordered and branching in many directions. It all makes sense to me, but short of teleportation to another mind how can I convey those ideas?
I have to restructure them to fit the sequential order and constraints of writing. In this process I can find out how much I really now about a subject, and even better, learn something new by the act of reordering thoughts. Writing is one of the best tools for thinking.
I am a thinker who loves to view ideas turned around in strange but strangely interesting perspectives.

Core Themes & Interests

  • Decentralization
  • Autonomy and self-sovereignty
  • Free and open-source software and hardware
  • Privacy, anti-censorship tools, and peer-to-peer systems
  • Cypherpunk principles
  • Permissionless innovation
  • Subversive ideas and speculative futures
